Back in 2018, a Brazilian e-commerce platform, Olist, published an anonymised dataset with information on nearly 100k orders placed over a period of 2 years (<a href="https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Kaggle</a>). The data covers customer location and purchase behaviour; order contents, payment, and delivery; as well as online ratings and reviews.

<br>

My project approaches it from the perspective of the end of the dataset - the middle of Q3 2018 - and asks what was the company's position at that time compared to the past, and what would have been the best course of action to make the rest of 2018 as successful as possible?

<br>

The analysis includes insights and recommendations on aspects such as:

- Revenue and order size
- Product category popularity
- Customer loyalty
- Customer satisfaction as reflected in ratings and review sentiments
- Delivery performance
- Local wins and concerns
